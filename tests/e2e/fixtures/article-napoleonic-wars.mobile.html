Napoleonic Wars

edit

The Napoleonic Wars (1803–1815) were a series of conflicts fought between the French Empire under Napoleon I and a fluctuating array of European coalitions.

The wars ended with the Bourbon restoration and a settlement at the Congress of Vienna that shaped Europe for a century.

Course of the wars

edit
  • 1805 — Austerlitz and Trafalgar.
  • 1812 — the invasion of Russia.
  • 1815 — Waterloo.
