HMS Victory is a 104-gun first-rate ship of the line of the Royal Navy, ordered in 1758 and launched in 1765. She is best known as Nelson's flagship at the Battle of Trafalgar in 1805.[1]

She has been preserved as a museum ship in Portsmouth since 1922 and remains the flagship of the First Sea Lord, making her the oldest commissioned warship in the world.

Serviceedit

Victory saw action at Ushant, Cape Spartel and Cape St Vincent before Trafalgar, and was rebuilt twice in her first forty years.
